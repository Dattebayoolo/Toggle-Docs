Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Toggle Docs and your local data.

Yes, 100% free forever. There are no subscriptions, hidden tiers, ads, or usage limits. Since Toggle Docs runs entirely on your local machine and uses zero server computing, there are no hosting costs passed to users.
Your documents are saved directly into your browser's persistent IndexedDB database on your physical device. You can also export individual documents to PDF, HTML, or plain text, as well as generate full workspace JSON backups whenever you wish.
Toggle Docs features a custom built-in phonetic transliteration engine. When you press Alt+U (or toggle the Urdu button in the toolbar), standard QWERTY keystrokes are automatically mapped to Urdu phonemes, rendered in authentic Noto Nastaliq calligraphy with proper RTL alignment.
Yes! Toggle Docs is a certified Progressive Web App (PWA). In Chrome, Edge, or Safari, simply click "Install Toggle Docs" or "Add to Home Screen" to install it as a standalone, distraction-free desktop or mobile application that works even without an internet connection.
